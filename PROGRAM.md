# Combined Training Program
### C25K + Knee Ability Zero + Low Back Ability + Gym Lifts

**TL;DR:** 6 training days built from 5 templates. Push, pull, and leg days carry the gym lifts, with LBA spine work bolted onto leg day and LBA upper-back work onto pull day. Your 3 weekly C25K runs use KAZ as the warm-up. Everything progresses on one rule: pain-free range first, then reps, then load.

---

## The Weekly Template

| Day | Session | Time |
|-----|---------|------|
| Day 1 | **Leg Day** + LBA spine + hips | 60-70 min |
| Day 2 | Full rest | - |
| Day 3 | **Run A** (C25K) + KAZ full session | 45-55 min |
| Day 4 | **Push Day** | 60-70 min |
| Day 5 | **Run B** (C25K) + KAZ lite | 35-40 min |
| Day 6 | **Pull Day** + LBA upper back | 50-60 min |
| Day 7 | **Run C** (C25K) + KAZ full session | 45-55 min |

**Why legs first, rest second:** running on legs still cooked from leg day is the highest-risk adjacency in this program, and the risk grows as C25K runs get longer. This order gives leg day a full rest day before any run. The only back-to-back at the cycle wrap (Run C into next cycle's legs) is the safe direction: running before legs, not after.

**Shift-schedule note:** These are templates, not calendar dates. The hard rules: keep at least one day between runs, keep the rest day after legs, and never a full run the day after leg day. Everything else slides freely around your rotation.

## Session A: Push Day (+ LBA Upper Back)

**Main lifts (current baselines, next targets):**

| Exercise | Current | Next Session |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Chest Press | 100 × 8 × 3 (reserve left) | 110 × 8 × 3 |
| Shoulder Press | 70 × 8 × 2 after ramp | 70 × 8 × 3, then 80 |
| Chest Fly | 75 × 17 final set | 85 × 12 × 3 |
| Lateral Raise | 10 × 15 × 2 | 12s × 12, or 10s × 15 × 3 |
| Skullcrusher / Band superset | 15 × 12-15, 3 rounds | Hold until all 3 rounds hit 15 |

**Progression rule:** When all working sets hit the top of the rep range with good form, add the smallest increment available next time. One lift at a time is fine.

**LBA upper-back work now lives on Pull Day (Session E), where it fits the movement pattern.**

## Session B: Leg Day (+ LBA Spine + Hips)

**LBA spine work FIRST (fresh, before fatigue):**
1. **Back Extension Iso Hold** - 2 holds, build toward 2:00 each
   - Level 1: bodyweight hold on floor (prone, chest lifted) or over a bench/GHD at partial range
   - Progress: 2-leg hold 2:00 → single-leg holds → slow full-range reps
   - Rule: mild working sensation is fine, sharp pain or spasm means regress one level
2. **4-Way Hip** - 1 round: hip flexor raises × 10/side, external rotations × 10/side, couch stretch 60s/side, squeeze-then-stretch adductors 60s

**Main lifts:**

| Exercise | Current | Next Session |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Leg Press | 208 × 10 × 3 | 208 × 10 × 3 again, then 220-230 |
| Ham Curl | 77 × 10 × 2 | 77 × 10 × 3, then add weight |
| Leg Extension | 55 × 10-12, 66 × 5 | 66 × 8 × 2-3 |
| Leg Press Calf Raise | (new, replacing standing calf) | Find a 12-15 rep weight, full stretch at bottom, pause 1s |

**Why leg press calf raises:** Your note was right, the standing machine cut off the stretch. Full stretch at the bottom is also exactly what KAZ wants for ankle/Achilles capacity, so this one change serves both programs.

## Session C: Run Day, Full (Runs A and C)

**Warm-up = KAZ Steps 1-4 (10-12 min):**
1. Backward walking - 5 min (parking lot, treadmill reverse, or field)
2. Tibialis raises - 25
3. FHL calf raises - 25
4. KOT calf raises - 25

**Then the C25K run for your current week** (see run schedule below)

**After the run, KAZ Steps 5-9 (12-15 min):**
5. Patrick step - 25/side, slow
6. ATG split squat - 5/side, regressed to pain-free range (elevate front foot as needed)
7. Elephant walk - 30 alternating reps
8. L-sit or seated leg raise - 6 × 10s
9. Couch stretch - 60s/side (skip if done on leg day within 24h, or do it anyway, it's free)

## Session D: Run Day, Lite (Run B)

**Warm-up:** Backward walking 5 min + tibialis raises 25 + KOT calf raises 25

**Then the C25K run**

**After:** Elephant walk × 30, couch stretch 60s/side. Done.

## Session E: Pull Day (+ LBA Upper Back)

New session, so the first one is calibration: find a weight you can move for clean sets of 10 with 2-3 reps left in the tank, log it, and that becomes your baseline.

**Main lifts:**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Starting Point |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| Lat Pulldown | 3 × 10 | Calibrate session 1 |
| Chest-Supported or Machine Row | 3 × 10 | Calibrate. Chest-supported keeps the low back out of it while LBA capacity builds |
| Single-Arm DB Row | 3 × 10/side | Calibrate, likely 25-35 lb |
| Rear Delt Fly (machine or DB) | 3 × 12-15 | Calibrate light |
| Biceps Superset × 3 | DB curl × 10-12 + band curl × 15 | Match your triceps superset structure |

**Row choice note:** Skip bent-over barbell rows for now. Chest-supported variations give the same back growth with zero spinal loading, which lets the LBA back extension progression be the ONLY thing challenging your low back directly. One variable at a time.

**LBA add-on (10 min, end of session):**
1. **DB Pullover** - 2 × 12-15, light (start 10-15 lb), thoracic extension and stretch focus, not load
2. **Trap-3 Raise** - 2 × 12-15/side, start bodyweight or 1-3 lb. Absurdly light is correct. Add 1 lb at a time.
3. **Dead Hang** - 2 × 20-30s if a bar is available. Grip work plus gentle spinal decompression, very LBA-friendly.

## C25K Run Schedule (9 Weeks)

All runs start with a 5-min brisk warm-up walk (your backward walking counts toward warming up but do 2-3 min forward brisk walk right before intervals).

| Week | Structure |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | 8 × (60s jog / 90s walk) |
| 2 | 6 × (90s jog / 2:00 walk) |
| 3 | 2 × (90s jog / 90s walk / 3:00 jog / 3:00 walk) |
| 4 | 3:00 jog, 90s walk, 5:00 jog, 2:30 walk, 3:00 jog, 90s walk, 5:00 jog |
| 5 | Varies by run; ends with a continuous 20:00 jog |
| 6 | Varies by run; ends with a continuous 25:00 jog |
| 7 | 3 × 25:00 continuous jogs |
| 8 | 3 × 28:00 continuous jogs |
| 9 | 3 × 30:00 continuous jogs (5K!) |

**Repeat rule:** If a week feels rough, repeat it. C25K weeks are checkpoints, not deadlines. Coming off a layoff, expect to repeat at least one week somewhere in weeks 4-6. That's normal.

## Post-Workout Stretch Blocks (5-8 min each)

All static holds, 30-60s each, done at the end of the session while warm. Breathe slow, sink deeper on exhales, no bouncing. Mild stretch sensation, never sharp.

**After Push Day:**
1. Doorway chest stretch - 45s (elbows at 90, step through)
2. Cross-body shoulder stretch - 30s/side
3. Overhead triceps + lat stretch - 30s/side (reach elbow behind head, lean sideways)
4. Thoracic extension over bench or foam roller - 45s (hands behind head, extend upper back only)

The thoracic one doubles as LBA work. Upper back extension is half of what the pullovers are chasing.

**After Leg Day:**
1. Standing or lying quad stretch - 45s/side (skip if couch stretch already done in the hip block, it covers the same tissue harder)
2. Seated or standing hamstring fold - 60s (relaxed rounding is fine and is actually the LBA point, let the spine bend gently)
3. Figure-4 glute stretch - 45s/side (lying on back, ankle over opposite knee)
4. Calf stretch on a step or wall - 45s/side, straight knee then bent knee

**After Pull Day:**
1. Dead hang or overhead lat stretch on a rack - 30s (doubles as decompression)
2. Child's pose with side reach - 45s/side
3. Upper trap stretch - 30s/side (ear toward shoulder, gentle)
4. Doorway biceps stretch - 30s/side (arm extended behind on frame)

**After Run Days (Full):**
KAZ Steps 5-9 already are the stretch block. Elephant walk, couch stretch, and split squats cover hamstrings, hips, and knees. Add only:
1. Calf stretch on a step - 45s/side
2. Figure-4 glute stretch - 30s/side

**After Run Days (Lite):**
The elephant walk and couch stretch in the finisher cover it. Add the step calf stretch (45s/side) and you're done.

**One rule from Backstrom that applies here:** stretching is loading too. If the back is cranky, gentle range beats aggressive range. A relaxed 60% stretch held calmly does more than a forced 100% stretch you brace against.

## Progression Philosophy (All Three Systems Agree)

- **Range before load.** KAZ and LBA both progress by earning deeper pain-free range first, then adding reps, then weight.
- **Never train through sharp pain.** Mild muscle burn and working sensation: fine. Sharp, shooting, or spasm: regress one level immediately.
- **Play the long game.** Backstrom's rule. Small increments (1-5 lb) every session beat big jumps every month. Your push day log already shows you doing this right.
- **Flare-ups are data.** If knees or back complain after a session, note what changed (volume, weight, running surface) and pull that variable back.
